Questions

Before you sign up.

Who is this for?+

University students, recent graduates and early- to mid-career professionals who want to work on hard, real-world problems before the stakes are real. No particular degree is required. Curiosity and the willingness to work in a team under pressure are.

What does “immersive” actually mean?+

Each weekend is a live scenario. Coaches and guests play the suppliers, regulators, executives and customers you have to deal with. Information arrives late and incomplete, and the situation changes while you work, just as it does in a real company.

How much time does it take?+

Four in-person weekends across the year, plus light team sprints and debriefs in between: 40 hours per month. It’s designed to fit alongside study or a full-time job.

Are the scenarios about real companies?+

They’re inspired by real cases across hardware, deep tech, energy and regulated industries, rebuilt so you face the same kinds of decisions. Sponsor companies may also author scenarios drawn from their own operations.

Can my employer pay?+

Yes. We can invoice your employer for individual or team seats. Ask about sponsorship if your company wants to author a scenario or recruit from the cohort.
